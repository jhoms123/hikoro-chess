    THE LITTLE CITADEL

    How to play

    Two armies of eight pieces face each other on a 5 × 5 board. Ember moves first. Select a friendly piece, then a highlighted square.

    Movement

    Sovereign: one square in any direction. Castellan: slide horizontally or vertically. Archer: slide diagonally. Sliding pieces stop at the first piece and may capture an enemy there. Cavalier: a 2 + 1 knight jump over intervening pieces. Squire: one square forward into empty space; capture one square diagonally forward. Ember moves toward row 5, Ivory toward row 1.

    Victory

    Capture the opposing Sovereign to win. The final position stays visible. There is no check, checkmate, castling, pawn double move, promotion, or dropping captured pieces. A Squire at the far edge has no further forward moves. No legal moves, threefold repetition, or the 1,000-ply limit draws.

    Play modes

    Share this device, play Ember against the bot, or join an online room from the collection. Local progress saves automatically when browser storage is available. Undo against the bot takes you back to your previous turn; redo restores the recorded reply. Online play verifies turns and moves on the server, restores your private seat after refresh, and disables undo. Online rooms are saved by the server when durable storage is enabled.

    Arrow keys navigate the board; Enter or Space selects; Escape clears selection. Flip board changes the view, not the rules.
