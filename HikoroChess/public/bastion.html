    BASTION MARCHES · A TERRITORY GAME IN THE HIKORO COLLECTION
    BASTION MARCHES

    How to play

    Object & setup

    Black and White each begin with two Keeps; Black moves first. On a 19 × 19 board, Black’s Keeps stand at D4 and P16, while White’s stand at P4 and D16. On a 13 × 13 board they stand at D4 and J10, mirrored for White. Keeps cannot move. Each player has four Marshals, eight Wardens, and an unlimited supply of Levies. White receives 7.5 komi.

    One action each turn

    Choose one action: raise a piece; move a piece or Warden stack; Embark one adjacent Levy onto a Warden; Disembark one or two carried Levies onto empty orthogonal neighbors; call settlement; or resign. There is no pass. A move ends on an empty intersection. Units do not capture by landing on enemies; capture happens by surrounding a group.

    Raise pieces

    Each new piece is placed on an empty point, within Manhattan distance of a friendly piece one rank above it: a Marshal within 4 of a Keep, a Warden within 3 of a Marshal, or a Levy within 2 of a Warden. Only one piece can be raised per turn. A Keep or Warden stack may support deployments as long as it remains on the board. A captured Marshal or Warden is gone for the game.

    Move, carry, and unload

    A Marshal slides 1–3 spaces along a row or column and cannot cross occupied points. A Warden moves one step in any of eight directions. A Levy moves one step orthogonally. A Warden carries up to two Levies; Embark moves an adjacent Levy onto it, and a stack moves using the Warden’s movement. Disembark places one or two carried Levies on distinct empty orthogonal neighbors. A moved piece or unloaded group must leave its friendly groups with liberties.

    Groups, liberties & capture

    Pieces of the same realm connect orthogonally. A group’s liberties are its adjacent empty points. When all liberties are filled, remove the whole group; a captured stack loses its Warden and every carried Levy. A move that leaves your own group without liberties is illegal unless capturing an opposing group restores a liberty. Edges are not liberties. Repeating a prior board, reserve, and next-player position is illegal.

    Besiege a Keep

    A group containing a Keep is a Fort. Its last liberty can be filled only when an attacking Marshal is orthogonally adjacent to every Keep in that Fort. Arrange the Marshal before taking the last liberty. A captured Keep is lost, but losing Keeps alone does not decide the game.

    Settle & score

    When the board is resolved, call settlement. Your opponent may accept and score, or contest by making one legal board action; play returns to the caller afterward. If accepted, every group still on the board counts as alive. Each occupied intersection is 1 point, including a stack. Each connected empty region touching pieces of only one realm belongs to that realm; neutral regions score nothing. Add 7.5 to White. More points wins. Resignation awards the win to the opponent.
