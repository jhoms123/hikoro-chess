The pieces

Dog · 7 each

Forward one or two squares along a clear file, or one square diagonally in any direction.

Hunter · 3 each

One square in any orthogonal direction. Capture two enemy hunters to win.

Child · 2 each

One square in any direction, or up to two clear squares along a diagonal.

Dragon · 1 each

Slides like a rook and leaps three forward plus one sideways. A friendly piece blocks its forward leap.

RULES & PIECE ART

How to play

Capture by surrounding

A piece is captured when two opposing pieces stand on opposite neighboring squares around it: across a rank, file, or diagonal. The move that completes the pair triggers capture. Dogs captured this way switch sides; captured hunters and children leave the board. Dragons cannot be captured.

Dragon displacement

A Dragon can land on any enemy non-Dragon piece if a separate friendly piece is orthogonally next to that target before the Dragon lands. The Dragon itself cannot provide the support. The target is removed, including dogs; displacement never flips a dog. Capturing a hunter counts toward the win.

Dragon pins

A Dragon may land on a piece and pin it beneath itself. The small icon badge shows what is underneath; the pinned piece cannot move until the Dragon leaves. Dragons cannot occupy a square the other Dragon could legally reach. Friendly pieces block their Dragon’s forward leap.

Winning & repetition

Capture two of the opposing hunters to win. A piece cannot make the same two-square back-and-forth loop for a third time; its third completion must be a different move. Moss moves first.
