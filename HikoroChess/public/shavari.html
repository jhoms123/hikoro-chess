THE SHAVARI TABLE

How to play

Two courts play on the intersections of a 9 × 9 grid. Carnelian moves first. Select a friendly formation, then a highlighted destination.

Movement

The Camel (Caravan lance) moves exactly two intersections diagonally in any direction. It cannot land on the intermediate intersection; an equal or taller formation there blocks its path. The Lotus General always moves alone: it steps one intersection like a king except for its two forward diagonals, with four orthogonal steps and two backward diagonals. Forward follows the General’s original court (Carnelian toward row 9, Turquoise toward row 1), even after flipping the board. The Scarab pawn steps orthogonally, and the Elephant cannon slides orthogonally. Non-General pieces in a formation contribute their movement, including enemy pieces underneath yours. A General never joins a formation. A moving formation may pass over shorter formations along its movement path. Equal or taller formations block further travel. A cannon does not require a screen.

Stacking & splitting

The top piece’s owner controls the whole formation; movement combines every piece being carried. Whole stack carries all members, Top piece detaches one, and Top two carries the upper pair. Non-General formations may stack on a friendly formation up to three pieces. A General always stays alone and cannot land on a friendly formation. Base members stay beneath arriving pieces. A moving formation can jump over another formation only when the moving portion is taller. Two pieces can pass over one; one cannot pass over two.

Capture or cover

On an enemy destination, Capture removes the whole enemy formation. Cover is available only when your moving portion is taller, neither formation contains a General, and the combined height is at most three. The enemy pieces stay underneath yours and contribute their movement. A General cannot be covered or stacked; capture it alone instead. Captures remain available at any enemy formation height. Friendly stacking follows the same limits.

Victory & draws

Choosing Capture removes every piece in the enemy formation, regardless of height. Capture the opposing Lotus General to win; a General can never be stacked or buried. There is no check, checkmate, promotion, or dropping captured pieces. If the player to move has no legal move, that player loses and the last mover wins. Threefold repetition, 100 consecutive plies without a capture, and the 4,000-ply safety limit are draws. A capture resets the 100-ply clock.

At the table

Local play shares this device and saves your match automatically when browser storage is available. Undo and redo work locally. Online matches use server-verified moves, restore your seat on refresh, and disable undo. Online rooms are saved by the server when durable storage is enabled.

The small board emblems show every stack member. Select a formation to see its members in the sidebar.

ENEMY FORMATION

Capture or cover?

Capture removes the enemy formation. Cover keeps its pieces beneath yours, adds their movement, and gives your top piece control.
