THE SHAVARI TABLE

How to play

Two courts play on the intersections of a 9 × 9 grid. Carnelian moves first. Select a friendly formation, then a highlighted destination.

Movement

The Camel (Caravan lance) moves exactly two intersections diagonally in any direction. It cannot land on the intermediate intersection; an equal or taller formation there blocks its path. The Lotus general moves one step like a king except for its two forward diagonals: four orthogonal steps and two backward diagonals. Forward is toward the opposite court from that piece’s original starting side (Carnelian toward row 9, Turquoise toward row 1), even inside a mixed stack or after flipping the board. The Scarab pawn steps orthogonally, and the Elephant cannon slides orthogonally. Every carried piece contributes its movement, including enemy pieces underneath yours. A moving formation may pass over shorter formations along its movement path. Equal or taller formations block further travel. A cannon does not require a screen.

Stacking & splitting

The top piece’s owner controls the whole formation; movement combines every piece being carried. Whole stack carries all members, Top piece detaches one, and Top two carries the upper pair. Land on a friendly formation to stack, with a maximum of three pieces. Base members stay beneath arriving pieces. A moving formation can jump over another formation only when the moving portion is taller. Two pieces can pass over one; one cannot pass over two.

Capture or cover

On an enemy destination, Capture removes the whole enemy formation. If your moving portion is taller and the combined height is at most three, you may instead choose Cover: the enemy pieces stay underneath yours and contribute their movement. Two pieces can cover one; one cannot cover two or one. Friendly stacking still allows any heights that fit within three.

Victory & draws

Choosing Capture on an enemy formation removes every piece in that formation. Capture the opposing Lotus general to win, even if it is buried in a stack. Covering a general does not capture it. Capturing your own general inside an enemy-controlled formation loses the match; capturing both generals draws. There is no check, checkmate, promotion, or dropping captured pieces. Three repetitions of the same board and side to move, no legal moves, or the 4,000-ply room limit end in a draw.

At the table

Local play shares this device and saves your match automatically when browser storage is available. Undo and redo work locally. Online matches use server-verified moves, restore your seat on refresh, and disable undo. A server restart clears online rooms.

The small board emblems show every stack member. Select a formation to see its members in the sidebar.

ENEMY FORMATION

Capture or cover?

Capture removes the enemy formation. Cover keeps its pieces beneath yours, adds their movement, and gives your top piece control.
