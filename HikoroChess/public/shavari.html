THE SHAVARI TABLE

How to play

Two courts play on the intersections of a 9 × 9 grid. Carnelian moves first. Select a friendly formation, then a highlighted destination.

Movement

The Lotus general and Scarab pawn move one intersection horizontally or vertically, including backwards. The Caravan lance and Elephant cannon slide any distance along a horizontal or vertical line. All pieces stop at the first occupied intersection; none jump or use a cannon screen.

Stacking & splitting

A formation’s top piece determines its movement. Whole stack moves every member; Top piece detaches only the uppermost member. Land on a friendly formation to stack, with a maximum of three pieces. Base members stay beneath arriving pieces. A stack cannot move through another formation.

Victory & draws

Landing on an enemy formation captures every piece in that formation. Capture the opposing Lotus general to win, even if it is buried in a stack. There is no check, checkmate, promotion, or dropping captured pieces. Three repetitions of the same board and side to move, no legal moves, or the 4,000-ply room limit end in a draw.

At the table

Local play shares this device and saves your match automatically when browser storage is available. Undo and redo work locally. Online matches use server-verified moves, restore your seat on refresh, and disable undo. A server restart clears online rooms.

Use arrow keys to navigate intersections, Enter or Space to select, and Escape to clear your selection. Flip board changes your view without changing the position.
