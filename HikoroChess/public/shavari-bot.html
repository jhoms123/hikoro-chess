Quick rules

Lotus General (G) steps orthogonally or backward diagonally. Capture a General to win.

Caravan Lance (L) jumps exactly two diagonals, provided intervening stacks are shorter than the moving portion.

Elephant Cannon (C) slides in four straight directions; Scarab Pawn (P) steps orthogonally.

Stacking and special outcomes

Carry one, two, or three pieces. Different pieces combine their moves. A stack may reach height three, except any stack containing a General is limited to height two. A General may stack with only one other piece. Taller formations jump over shorter blockers; equal or higher stacks stop them. A moving stack may cover a smaller enemy stack if the resulting height is legal (maximum three, or two when a General is present). Capturing eliminates every member, including buried Generals. Capturing your own General loses. Capturing both draws. After 100 consecutive plies without a capture, the game draws; a capture resets the clock. Threefold repetition, immobilization, or the 4000-ply cap also draw.
