    YOUR GO VARIANT

    Old stones. New possibilities.

    Leap

    A stone jumps two points orthogonally over an enemy stone, capturing it. Further legal jumps form a chain. Stop after any jump by making that stone a shield; shielding is required to finish the turn.

    Shield

    Use a turn and a reserve stone to transform a stone. The shield moves one point in any direction and cannot be captured by jumping.

    Surround

    Stones and shields share liberties in connected groups. Surround a group to capture it. Two passes settle the score.

    SHIELD GO

    How to play

    Black starts on an empty 9 × 9 or 13 × 13 board. Each player has 100 reserve stones. Placing a stone or making a shield spends one reserve stone; movement spends none. On your turn, place a stone on an empty point, jump with a stone, move a shield, turn a stone into a shield, or pass.

    Stones & shields

    Ordinary stones only move by jumping two points horizontally or vertically over an opposing ordinary stone into an empty point. The jumped stone is captured. After every jump, you keep the same turn until the jumping stone becomes a shield. If another legal jump is available, continue with that same stone or choose Make shield to stop the chain early. If no further jump is available, you must choose Make shield. You cannot pass, place a stone, or move or shield another piece during a chain.

    Make shield transforms a selected ordinary stone permanently, spends one reserve stone, and ends your turn. After a jump or chain, that transformation is mandatory for the jumping stone. Shields move one point in any of eight directions to an empty point. They cannot jump or be jumped, but can be captured by surrounding.

    Liberties & captures

    A group connects orthogonally, including friendly stones and shields. Its liberties are adjacent empty points. Groups with no liberties are captured. A placement or movement that leaves your own group without liberties is illegal. There is no immediate ko prohibition; If the same board with the same player to move occurs three times, the match ends and the higher score wins; a tied score remains a draw.

    Scoring

    Two consecutive passes or either player using their last reserve stone finish the match. Ordinary stones score 1 point, shields score ½ point, each captured opposing piece scores 1 point, and empty territory bordered only by your color scores 1 point per intersection. White receives 4 komi. There is no automatic dead-stone removal. Settle disputed groups before passing. Score totals during play are provisional.
