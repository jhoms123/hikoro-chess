Go-style territory · Human or AI

Shavari Go

A royal territory game on a 13 × 13 board. Place your court, extend its banners, and surround rival groups. Pieces never move: every turn changes the frontier.

13 × 13 only · Local two-player, play a bot, or watch two bots.

Opening: place the Crownholds

Each side chooses two empty intersections for its Crownholds. Black places first, then White, then Black, then White. These four opening moves are compulsory; after the fourth, regular play starts with Black. A bot places its own Crownholds.

The royal court · per player

Every piece stays where placed. No movement or stacking.
Crownhold
2 · opening strongholds

Choose any two empty points for your opening Crownholds. If their connected group is surrounded, the attacker needs a High Marshal beside every Crownhold in that group to capture it.

High Marshal
4 · siege commander

Beside every Crownhold in a surrounded group, the Marshal unlocks its capture. Against a Royal Guard group, touching any one member is enough.

Royal Guard
6 · guarded group

A surrounded group containing a Guard needs an enemy High Marshal orthogonally touching any member of that whole group before it can be captured.

Standard-Bearer
4 · orthogonal lines

Creates continuous horizontal and vertical placement lines through empty points. A line stops at the board edge or the first occupied point.

Crossbowman
4 · diagonal lines

Creates continuous diagonal placement lines through empty points. A line stops at the board edge or the first occupied point.

Squire
169 · basic piece

Your plentiful basic piece. Place it orthogonally beside a friendly piece or anywhere reached by your Standard-Bearer and Crossbowman lines.

After the opening: place one piece from your reserve on an empty point orthogonally beside a friendly piece, or along any open line projected by one of your Standard-Bearers or Crossbowmen. These lines continue through empty points and stop at the first occupied point. Any piece can use any open line. Capture: same-color pieces joined orthogonally form a group. Its liberties are adjacent empty points. Surrounding all liberties captures the group, except a Royal Guard group needs a High Marshal touching any member, and a Crownhold group needs an enemy High Marshal beside every Crownhold it contains. Suicide is illegal unless the placement first captures an enemy group. Captured non-Crownholds return to their owner's reserve; Crownholds are lost. Movement: there is none.

    Full rules & bot thinking

    Object & score. Take more area than your opponent. Each occupied intersection is one point. Empty connected regions touching only one side also score for that side; neutral regions score neither. White gets 7.5 komi. Two consecutive passes end the game. Repeating the immediately previous board position is forbidden.

    Opening. Black and White each place two Crownholds on any empty points, alternating Black–White–Black–White. These are each side's first two moves. Black takes the first regular turn after the four Crownholds are placed. Bots choose their own Crownhold positions.

    Placement & capture. On each regular turn place one piece on an empty point orthogonally beside a friendly piece, or along any open ray from a friendly Standard-Bearer (orthogonal) or Crossbowman (diagonal). Rays pass across empty points and stop at the board edge or first occupied point. Same-side pieces connect orthogonally. A group with no adjacent empty points is surrounded and captured; suicide is illegal unless the move captures first.

    Siege. A group containing one or more Crownholds may only be captured when an attacking High Marshal is orthogonally next to every Crownhold in the group. A group containing a Royal Guard may only be captured when an attacking High Marshal is orthogonally next to any member of that group. If both conditions apply, both must be met. Captured non-Crownholds return to reserve; Crownholds are permanently lost.

    Turns & end. Pieces never move or stack. You may pass after the Crownhold opening. Two consecutive passes score the position. Resignation ends the match. Area score plus komi decides the winner.
