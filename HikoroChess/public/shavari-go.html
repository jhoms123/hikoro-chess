    Full rules & bot thinking

    Object & score. Claim more territory. Only empty connected regions touching pieces of one side score for that side; regions touching both sides are neutral. Occupied intersections do not score, and captured pieces returning to reserve are not prisoners for scoring. White gets 3.5 komi. Two consecutive passes end scoring. Capturing a Crownhold or resignation ends immediately. Repeating the immediately previous board position is forbidden.

    Opening. Black and White each place two Crownholds on any empty points, alternating Black–White–Black–White. Black takes the first regular turn after the four Crownholds are placed. Bots choose their own Crownhold positions.

    Ordinary placement. Place one reserve piece on an empty point orthogonally beside a friendly piece or along an active ray from a friendly Standard-Bearer (orthogonal) or Crossbowman (diagonal). Rays pass through empty points and stop at the first occupied point. An enemy Sentry orthogonally beside either ray piece silences all of that piece’s long lines; adjacent placement remains legal.

    Special placements. A Sapper may be placed only on the first empty point immediately beyond exactly one enemy piece on an active friendly ray. It cannot cross a friendly piece or a second enemy. A Cavalier may use a normal route or make a chess-knight L placement from any friendly piece, ignoring intervening pieces. Each side has two Sappers, two Cavaliers, and two Sentries.

    Capture & siege. Same-side pieces joined orthogonally form a group with adjacent empty intersections as liberties. A group with no liberties is captured; suicide is illegal unless the move first captures an enemy group. A Royal Guard group needs an attacking High Marshal beside any member. A group containing Crownholds needs a High Marshal beside every Crownhold it contains. Capturing any Crownhold immediately ends the game and wins for the attacker. Captured non-Crownholds return to their owner’s reserve; Crownholds are permanently lost.

    Turns & end. Pieces never move or stack. You may pass after the Crownhold opening. Two consecutive passes score the position; resignation ends the match.

    Moss · Builder. Values broad influence, safe connections, and expanding placement networks. Slate · Tactician. Searches for captures, siege threats, Sapper breaches, Cavalier jumps, and Sentry line denial.

    The influence overlay helps read the board; it does not affect scoring.

    Meet the royal courtNine roles · placement, siege, and counterplay

    Crownhold

    2 · opening strongholds

    Choose two empty points. Its connected group needs an enemy Marshal beside every Crownhold to be captured. Capturing any Crownhold immediately wins the game.

    High Marshal

    4 · siege commander

    Unlocks guarded captures. Touch any member of a Royal Guard group, or each Crownhold in a surrounded group.

    Royal Guard

    6 · guarded group

    Its connected group resists a surround until an enemy Marshal touches any member of that group.

    Standard-Bearer

    4 · orthogonal lines

    Projects placement along open horizontal and vertical lines. An orthogonally adjacent enemy Sentry silences its projected lines, but not ordinary adjacent placement.

    Crossbowman

    4 · diagonal lines

    Projects placement along open diagonals. An orthogonally adjacent enemy Sentry silences its projected lines, but not ordinary adjacent placement.

    Sapper

    2 · breach placement

    Place on the first empty point immediately beyond exactly one enemy on an active Standard-Bearer or Crossbowman line. Friendly pieces and a second enemy stop the breach.

    Cavalier

    2 · knight leap

    May use any ordinary placement route or leap in a chess-knight L from any friendly piece, ignoring intervening pieces.

    Sentry

    2 · silences lines

    An enemy Standard-Bearer or Crossbowman orthogonally beside a Sentry loses its long placement lines. Those lines return if the Sentry is captured.

    Squire

    169 · basic pieces

    The plentiful basic piece. It can use the same friendly adjacency or projected line as every other rank.

    How to play

    1. Open your court. Each player places two Crownholds on any empty intersections, alternating turns.
    2. Place a piece. After the opening, place beside a friendly piece or along an active Standard-Bearer or Crossbowman line. A Sapper breaches exactly one enemy and lands in the first empty point beyond it. A Cavalier can leap in a knight’s L from a friendly piece.
    3. Use the Sentry. A Sentry orthogonally beside an enemy Standard-Bearer or Crossbowman silences that piece’s long placement lines. It can still place next to a friendly piece.
    4. Surround a group. A group with no liberties is captured. A Guard group needs an adjacent attacking Marshal; each Crownhold in a surrounded group needs its own adjacent attacking Marshal. Capturing any Crownhold immediately wins.
    5. Claim territory. Only empty regions bordered by one side score; stones do not. White receives 3.5 komi. Two passes end the scoring game.

    Pieces stay where placed. Tap a highlighted intersection after choosing a piece. On a phone, use Enlarge board for more precise placement.
