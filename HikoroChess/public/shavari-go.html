    Full rules & bot thinking

    Object & score. Take more area than your opponent. Each occupied intersection is one point. Empty connected regions touching only one side also score for that side; neutral regions score neither. White gets 7.5 komi. Two consecutive passes end the game. Repeating the immediately previous board position is forbidden.

    Opening. Black and White each place two Crownholds on any empty points, alternating Black–White–Black–White. Black takes the first regular turn after the four Crownholds are placed. Bots choose their own Crownhold positions.

    Placement & capture. On each regular turn place one piece on an empty point orthogonally beside a friendly piece, or along any open ray from a friendly Standard-Bearer (orthogonal) or Crossbowman (diagonal). Rays pass across empty points and stop at the board edge or first occupied point. Same-side pieces connect orthogonally. A group with no adjacent empty points is surrounded and captured; suicide is illegal unless the move captures first.

    Siege. A group containing one or more Crownholds may only be captured when an attacking High Marshal is orthogonally next to every Crownhold in the group. A group containing a Royal Guard may only be captured when an attacking High Marshal is orthogonally next to any member of that group. If both conditions apply, both must be met. Captured non-Crownholds return to reserve; Crownholds are permanently lost.

    Turns & end. Pieces never move or stack. You may pass after the Crownhold opening. Two consecutive passes score the position. Resignation ends the match. Area score plus komi decides the winner.

    Moss · Builder. Values broad influence, safe connections, and expanding placement networks. Slate · Tactician. Searches more deeply around captures, Marshal siege threats, and vulnerable groups.

    How to play

    1. Open your court. Each player places two Crownholds on any empty intersections, alternating turns.
    2. Place a piece. After the opening, place next to one of your pieces or along an open line from a Standard-Bearer or Crossbowman.
    3. Surround a group. Remove a connected enemy group when it has no empty orthogonal neighbors. A Guard group needs an adjacent attacking Marshal; each Crownhold in a surrounded group needs its own adjacent attacking Marshal.
    4. Claim area. Pieces and empty enclosed intersections score one point each. White receives 7.5 komi. Two passes end the match.

    Pieces stay where placed. Tap a highlighted intersection after choosing a piece. On a phone, use Enlarge board for more precise placement.
