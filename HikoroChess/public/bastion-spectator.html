    What the bots are looking for

    Territory and influence. The bots estimate Go-style influence across open intersections, then compare it with secure territory.

    Liberties and eyes. Groups with few liberties are at risk. Connected groups and protected eyes are harder to capture.

    Invasion and connection. Wardens extend the Levy network; moves are judged for both expansion and cutting off weak groups.

    Siege and tactics. The search considers the opponent’s best reply. A Fort is only capturable when an attacking Marshal is beside every Keep in it.

    The bot is a strategic demonstrator, not a solved engine. Its move score is an estimate; only the settlement score decides the winner.
